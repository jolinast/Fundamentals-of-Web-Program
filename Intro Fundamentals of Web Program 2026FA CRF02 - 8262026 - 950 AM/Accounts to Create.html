Required Now

Kirkwood Email

Log into your student email via MyHub using your k-number and password. Use the Student Email link to automatically log you in to your email account.

GitHub

Sign up using your personal email because you will likely use this during your career. When asked how you will use GitHub, select things related to student projects. 

Codepen

Sign up for Codepen using your GitHub account. I will use this tool to demonstrate front-end web design concepts and will ask you to copy the starter code to make improvements.

CodeSandbox

Sign up for CodeSandbox using your GitHub account. I will also use this tool to demonstrate front-end web design concepts. This would be a great tool to use during pair programming activities.

Required Later

Student Developer Pack

First, go to your GitHub settings and add your "@student.kirkwood.edu" school email as a second email address.

Second, enable 2-factor authentication for your account.

You may be asked to add billing information with your full legal name as it appears on your academic affiliation documentation. You do not have to add a payment method.

Lastly, apply for the Student Developer Pack. Click the green "Start an application" button.

Fill out the "Education Benefits Application" form and share your location.

When asked for evidence of student status, take a picture of your student ID with a sheet of paper that shows the current year. 

Wait a few minutes for the automated system to approve your application. Your benefits will expire in two years. If your application is declined, try again or ask for help.

Whimsical

Whimsical allows users to easily build flowcharts, wireframes, mind maps, sequence diagrams and more. Students and teachers can use Whimsical free of charge by filling out this form to apply.

Miro

Fill in the application form to apply for free student access to Miro.

When asked, "What will you use Miro for?" say something like "Class Web Design Projects."

Optional AI Tools

GitHub Copilot

While signed in to GitHub, go to Copilot features and enable Copilot Pro on the account. You should see a message saying you are eligible to use GitHub Copilot for free.

On the "Select your preferences" page, you might want to enable options for "Copilot can search the web".

Cursor

Apply for a free student Cursor account.

Replit

Sign up for a free Replit account. After signing in, optionally clicking the "Upgrade to Replit Core" will offer students $10/month for 6 months or $100 off an annual plan.
