What You Will Learn

The focus of this course is on web design and development using HTML and CSS. Students will gain an understand the software development life cycle, requirements gathering, and wireframing. 

Students will use software such as Firebase Studio, Figma, and Webflow to create digital assets, design layouts, and write code.

Check out the 2025 Stack Overflow Developer Survey to see how HTML and CSS stack up with the competition. Spoiler alert: JavaScript, HTML/CSS, SQL, and Python are at the top.

Informatics, Inc. is a popular Digital Marketing and Web Development company in Cedar Rapids that works with variety of web development content management tools:

  • WordPress - CR Metro Economic Alliance, Hawkeye Ready Mix
  • Concrete - ImOn Communications, HACAP, CR Museum of Art
  • Shopify - Midamar Halal, their main website is made using Squarespace
  • .NET - Second Story Promotions
  • Laravel - Cause Team

I used https://whatcms.org/ to figure out .NET and Laravel. 

In our class, the instructor will use this site as an example while demonstrating code samples. Many in-class activities and assignments will come from the Frontend Mentor website.

Students will choose one website (preferably an actual local business from the Informatics website) to recreate for their final project. The project can be done individually or with a partner. No more than three students may be in a group.

Another option is to find a modern website template and recreate it. Here are a few good examples:

Test-Out Exams

Kirkwood's Business and IT department offers a test-out exam for students who come to this course with previous experience and can meet the previously stated outcomes. 

The exams must be taken in one of Kirkwood's test centers. There are no books, notes, or other resources allowed for the exams. Contact business@kirkwood.edu for more information.

Courses that offer test-out exams include:

DFW Rates

This course has one of the highest Drop-Fail-Withdrawal rates. In the fall of 2025, the rate was 40.5%. My goal is to reduce DFW Rates through choosing real-world assignments mapped to the Course Student Learning Outcomes (CSLOs) and require bi-monthly one-on-one meetings for homework assistance.
