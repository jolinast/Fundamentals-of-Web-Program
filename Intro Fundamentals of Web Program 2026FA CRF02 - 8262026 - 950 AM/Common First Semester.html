Fundamentals of Web Programming is used as part of Kirkwood's common first semester for I.T. programs. It is also generally available to students in other programs and is offered in high schools and academies.

Computer Software Development

Computer Software Development

For Computer Software Development (CSD) students, modern applications often take advantage of web technologies for user interfaces.  As a result, CSD students will need to understand these fundamentals just as well as the Web Application Development students.  Also, the discussions of site owners, visitors, and their reasons for interacting are very important in that it matches the CSD use case lessons.

Computer Support Specialist

Computer Support Specialist (CSS)

For Computer Support Specialist (CSS) students, the technical need is lower.  In their career, they will almost never create HTML or CSS,  However, they will be supporting people who experience issues with websites.  The more knowledge the CSS person has, the better they can do in those situations.  Again, the site owner and visitor information will be extremely useful.

Networking and System Administration

Network and Systems Administration (NASA)

For the Networking and System Administration (NaSA) students, they will be creating and maintaining the environments where all the other students will end up working.  A basic understanding of web technology and web pages will be useful in that capacity.  The site owner and visitor concepts will be useful here just like the others.

Non-IT Students

The course is open to anyone. These students are typically curious about how web development applies to their careers. For example, an graphic design student who takes this course will be far better equipped than their peers to take the designs they create and turn them into actual websites.
